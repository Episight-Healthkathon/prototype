# EPISIGHT interface

The user's supplied slide pins the visual direction: white and pale blue surfaces, navy typography, blue and teal accents, generous margins and restrained rounded panels. The existing logo remains unchanged.

Landing mode: explain the signal → episode → evidence → brief mechanism through an interactive case story and a supplied illustration slide. One hero preview, an editorial workflow sequence and a scenario selector, rather than repetitive marketing cards.

Reviewer mode: persistent navigation, compact queue, chronological episode rail, explicit evidence gaps and a source drawer. Status uses text and icons alongside color. All patient identities are pseudonymous. Outcomes are evidence states, not claim decisions.

Typography: self-hosted Manrope with a readable system fallback. Navy #102747, blue #086bc4, teal #087a79, slate #52647b. Border radii 12–16px, compact control radius 8px. Motion is limited to the landing case transition and drawer entrance, respecting reduced motion.

Responsive: navigation becomes a compact top row; multi-column workspace becomes a readable stack. Tables retain horizontal scrolling inside their container. Focus outlines, keyboard controls, loading/error/empty states and source availability are part of the system.
